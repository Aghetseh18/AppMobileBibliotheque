# User Documentation: Library Mobile Application

Welcome to the **Library Mobile Application**, your all-in-one digital companion for academic excellence, book discovery, and collaborative learning. This guide provides detailed instructions on how to navigate and make the most of the application's features.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Welcome Page
When you first open the application, you are greeted by a **Welcome Page**. This gatekeeper screen offers two primary options to get you started:
- **Sign In**: For existing users to access their personalized dashboard.
- **Sign Up**: For new users to create their academic profile.

### 1.2 Account Creation (Sign Up)
If you are new to the platform:
1. Tap **Sign Up** on the welcome page.
2. Enter your details, including **Full Name**, **Email**, and a **Secure Password**.
3. Once completed, your account is created, and you will be automatically logged into the main application.

### 1.3 Accessing Your Account (Sign In)
If you already have an account:
1. Tap **Sign In** on the welcome page.
2. Enter your registered **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tap the login button to access your dashboard.

---

## 2. Main Dashboard & Navigation

The application is organised into four primary tabs located at the bottom of your screen:

- **🏠 Home**: Your main portal for book discovery and library catalog.
- **📚 E-learning**: Access to courses, materials, and academic resources.
- **💬 Chat**: Real-time communication with other users or staff.
- **⚙️ Settings**: Profile management, configuration, and app information.

### 2.1 The Header Utility Bar
At the top of most screens, you will find:
- **🔔 Notifications**: Tap the bell icon to view recent updates and alerts.
- **🛒 Cart**: View and manage books or resources you've added for purchase or checkout.
- **🔍 Global Search**: Quickly find books, authors, or course materials.

---

## 3. Library Features

### 3.1 Book Discovery
- **Popular Books**: Explore the most trending and highly-rated books in the library.
- **Recommendations**: Personalized suggestions based on your reading history and interests.
- **Similar Books**: When viewing a book, check this section to find related titles.

### 3.2 Book Details
Tap on any book to see detailed information:
- **Description & Author**: Learn more about the content and the author.
- **Availability**: Check if the book is available for physical checkout or digital reading.
- **Reviews**: Read feedback from other students.

### 3.3 Book Reservation
To reserve a book:
1. **Search**: Find the book you wish to borrow in the catalogue.
2. **Reserve**: Tap the **"Reserve"** button on the book details page.
3. **Confirmation**: You will receive a confirmation if the reservation is successful.
4. **Pickup**: Go to the library reception to pick up your book.
   - *Note*: You can have a maximum of **3 active reservations** at a time.
   - Check your current reservations in **Settings > My Borrowings**.

---

## 4. E-learning & OpenClassrooms

The **E-learning** tab provides a structured learning environment:
- **Departments & Semesters**: Navigate through your academic curriculum organized by department and semester.
- **Course Materials**: Access PDFs, slide decks, and lecture notes.
- **Quizzes**: Test your knowledge with integrated quizzes at the end of modules.

---

## 5. Communication & AI Assistance

### 5.1 Real-time Chat
Stay connected via the **Chat** tab. You can receive messages from professors, librarians, or fellow students regarding your book requests or academic tasks.

### 5.2 Gemini-Powered ChatBot
For instant help, use the **Floating ChatBot Icon** (orange bubble) available on the home screen:
- Ask questions about available books.
- Get help navigating the app.
- Ask for academic assistance or book summaries.
- The AI uses advanced RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) to provide accurate answers based on the library's catalog.

---

## 6. Profile & Settings

The **Settings** page is your central hub for personalization and account management. It contains the following entry points:

- **👤 Personal Information**: Modify your profile details, name, and contact information.
- **🔑 Change Password**: Secure your account by updating your password regularly.
- **📖 My Borrowings**: View a list of books you have currently checked out or reserved.
- **📜 Consultation History**: Review your past book searches and consulted materials.
- **🔔 Notifications**: Manage your alert preferences and view recent notifications.
- **🌐 Language**: Change the application interface language to your preference.
- **👥 Invite a Student**: Share the app with your peers to collaborate and learn together.
- **❓ Help and Support**: Access documentation, tutorials, or contact the technical team.
- **🔒 Privacy Settings**: Manage your data privacy and account visibility.
- **ℹ️ About App**: View version information, credits, and legal terms.

---

## 7. Troubleshooting & FAQs

**Q: I didn't receive a notification.**
A: Ensure that notifications are enabled in your device settings and within the app's **Notifications** menu under Settings.

**Q: How do I checkout a book?**
A: Add the book to your **Cart** and proceed to the checkout screen to confirm your request.

**Q: The ChatBot isn't responding.**
A: Check your internet connection. The AI assistant requires an active data connection to process requests.
