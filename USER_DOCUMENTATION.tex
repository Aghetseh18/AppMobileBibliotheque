\section*{Welcome}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Welcome}

Welcome to the \textbf{Library Mobile Application}, your all-in-one digital companion for academic excellence, book discovery, and collaborative learning. This guide provides detailed instructions on how to navigate and make the most of the application's features.

% ==================================================
\section{Getting Started}

\subsection{Welcome Page}
When you first open the application, you are greeted by a \textbf{Welcome Page}. This screen provides two main options:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Sign In}: For existing users to access their dashboard.
    \item \textbf{Sign Up}: For new users to create an academic profile.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.3\textwidth]{images/first page.jpeg}
    \caption{Welcome Page}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Creating an Account (Sign Up)}
If you are new to the platform:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Tap \textbf{Sign Up} on the welcome page.
    \item Enter your \textbf{Full Name}, \textbf{Email}, \textbf{Secure Password}, and other details.
    
    \begin{figure}[H]
        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=0.3\textwidth]{images/Register.jpeg}\hfill
        \includegraphics[width=0.3\textwidth]{images/register2.jpeg}
        \caption{Sign Up Process}
    \end{figure}

    \item Once completed, a verification email will be sent to you.
    
    \begin{figure}[H]
        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=0.3\textwidth]{images/Verification.jpeg}
        \caption{Email Verification}
    \end{figure}

    \item After verification, your account will be created, and you will be automatically logged in.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Accessing Your Account (Sign In)}
If you already have an account:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Tap \textbf{Sign In} on the welcome page.
    \item Enter your registered \textbf{Email} and \textbf{Password}.
    \item Tap the login button to access your Dashboard.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.3\textwidth]{images/signin.jpeg}
    \caption{Sign In Screen}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Book Reservation}
To reserve a book:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Search}: Find the book you wish to borrow in the catalogue.
    \item \textbf{Reserve}: Tap the \textbf{``Reserve''} button on the book details page.
    \item \textbf{Confirmation}: You will receive a confirmation if the reservation is successful.
    \item \textbf{Pickup}: Go to the library reception to pick up your book.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Troubleshooting \& FAQs}

\noindent\textbf{Q: I didn't receive a notification.}\\
Ensure notifications are enabled in both device settings and the app's \textbf{Notifications} menu.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Q: How do I checkout a book?}\\
Add the book to your \textbf{Cart} and confirm the checkout request.

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent\textbf{Q: The ChatBot isn't responding.}\\
Verify your internet connection, as the AI requires active connectivity.
